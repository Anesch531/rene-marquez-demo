About Rene

A Spanish coach,
a decade on court.

I'm a Spanish padel coach with more than a decade in the game — playing since 2013 in Spain, coaching clubs in Tenerife, and competing in Dubai tournaments since 2023.

In Spain I ran interclub teams as a head coach and trained players from first grip to competition level. In Dubai I play the game I teach — representing my sponsors on court every week.

Every private session is built around you: honest feedback, clear structure, and progress you can feel by the end of it.
